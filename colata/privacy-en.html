Privacy policy

Colata · updated 25 October 2026

In short
  • No account, no name or email: your game stays on your phone.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob: in the EU you choose whether they are personalised, and you can change your mind from the pause menu.
  • Under 13s cannot play; from 13 to 15, ads are non-personalised only.

Colata is a game for iPhone and Android. It has no accounts, never asks for your name or email and has no server of its own: everything about your game stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is collected by Google's advertising service (AdMob), as explained below.

Who is responsible

Simone Rigoni, email job.rigoni@gmail.com. Write to this address with any question about this policy.

What stays on your phone

  • Your save: scores and records, settings, pieces on the shelf, challenges and in-game stock, your latest games as replays. They stay in the app's storage and are never sent anywhere.
  • Month and year of birth: asked once, at the end of the first game, only to know whether you can play (not under 13) and which kind of ads to show. They stay on your phone and are not sent to anyone, not even to Google: AdMob is only told whether you should receive ads for minors.
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, they are notifications scheduled by the phone itself. No server is involved.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and paid for by ads, served through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK collects data from the phone such as IP address, device model and operating system, device identifiers and interactions with ads.

  • Consent. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before the first ad: personalised ads only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy e annunci" button in the game's pause menu.
  • On iPhone the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you say yes to Apple's "Allow this app to track you" question, which we only ask players aged 16 or over. You can change your answer in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Aged 13 to 15: ads are always non-personalised and the consent form does not appear: we tell Google to treat you as under the age of digital consent.
  • For everyone, ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.
  • Without a connection there are no ads, and the game works the same.

Google processes this data as an independent controller under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps. Google may transfer data outside the European Union, including to the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (Google participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

Legal bases

  • Personalised ads and access to device identifiers: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given in Google's form and, on iPhone, in Apple's prompt.
  • Non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention: as stated by Google in the consent form and in its policy.
  • Save data and date of birth on the phone: needed to run the game as you asked and to follow the rules on minors; they never leave the phone.

Children

Colata is for players aged 13 and over. Anyone who enters a lower age cannot play and receives no ads: in that case Google's SDK is not even started. From 13 to 15, ads are non-personalised only and we do not ask for tracking.

What we don't do

No accounts, no analytics of our own, no selling of data, no in-app purchases in this version.

Your rights

You have the rights granted by the GDPR (Articles 15-22): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent at any time. We keep no data about you outside your phone, so for ad data the most direct route is Google's tools (myadcenter.google.com) and the game's "Privacy e annunci" button. You can still write to us with any request. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority of your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with an updated date. Important changes involving new data will also be announced in the game.
